Fishstrap V2 is a custom bootstrapper for Roblox, rebuilt from scratch around a sleek, dark, dashboard-first interface — quick launch, quick settings and live stats on one screen.

Download latest View source

Everything, one click away

The essentials live on the dashboard; the power tools live one sidebar click deep.

Quick LaunchRoblox Player and Studio with your custom setup, right from the dashboard.
FastFlags editorUnhidden, with search, presets, JSON import/export and an allowlist mode.
Bootstrapper themesClassic Fluent, Terminal, TwentyFive, Bloxnified — or import any Bloxstrap-style theme.
Server info by RoValraSee the server you joined — location and uptime — while you play.
Version managementChannel switcher, version pinning, reinstall and old-version pruning.
ModsCustom cursors, sounds and fonts, applied on every launch.
Discord Rich PresenceShow your game in Discord, with activity tracking on the side.
Live statisticsLaunches and playtime with a 14-day activity chart.

The FastFlags editor

Search, presets, import/export — and it can't wipe what you can't see.
